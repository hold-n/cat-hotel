# Cat Hotel Website - Guiding Principles

> **Note to future agents**: Keep this file updated! When you make design decisions or learn new context about the project, document it here. Don't duplicate what's already in the HTML - focus on the "why" and things that aren't obvious from the code.

## Purpose
A half-joke, half-real landing page for "Cat Hotel" - a cat-sitting service run by Max & Mira in Dublin for friends. The site should be funny enough to share as a joke, but legitimate enough that friends might actually use it to arrange cat-sitting.

## Tone & Style
- **Cheesy and punny**: Cat puns encouraged (purrfect, meow, fur real, etc.)
- **Playful but not childish**: Humor should land with adults
- **Self-aware**: The site knows it's a bit ridiculous (e.g., form admits it does nothing)
- **Sketchy/careless aesthetic**: Handwritten fonts, dashed borders, casual feel
- **Based on reality**: Amenities and descriptions should reflect what Max & Mira actually have/do

## Design Principles
- **Minimalistic**: No gradients, shadows, or excessive animations
- **Flat colors**: Purple, brown, red/orange color scheme
- **Simple borders**: Dashed borders for cards, solid for emphasis
- **Mobile-friendly**: Must look good on phones
- **Favicon**: Keep it simple enough to read at 32x32 pixels

## Cat Photos
- Show the entire cat, never crop body parts
- Use `background-size: contain` to fit full cat in frame
- Pill-shaped containers work well for portrait photos
- Clicking photos opens full-size lightbox

## Real Hotel Details
Things that are actually true (use for authentic humor):
- Big window with tree and birds ("Bird TV")
- Bed, couch, chairs - but cats often prefer carpet
- Houseplants that cats eat despite being told not to
- No scratching post or laser pointer
